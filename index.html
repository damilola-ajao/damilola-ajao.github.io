About Me

From compliance to design

My name is Damilola Ajao and my interdisciplinary background has allowed me to bring a unique approach to product thinking. I studied Econometrics at the University of South Florida, spent three years in Compliance at Goldman Sachs (Asset & Wealth Management), and am now completing my master's in Information Studies at UT Austin.

Compliance has more in common with design than it seems. One of my first projects at Goldman was building self-paced training modules that broke dense financial regulations into short, engaging courses, with interactive visuals, easy navigation, and lots of research and testing to make them user-friendly. I loved every bit of it. That project introduced me to the kind of work I wanted to do more of, and it led me to UX. This role still shapes how I design today: I look for consistency, and I'm particular about the small details, because I've seen how much they add up.

I'm currently open to UX research and design roles where I can make complex problems feel simple.

Outside of work, you'll find me on the tennis court, at the table tennis table, or indulging my love for aesthetics and curated spaces, often as the unofficial interior designer for my friends and family. I also write about UX on Medium.
